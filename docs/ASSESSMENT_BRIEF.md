AI Engineer Assessment
Digital Marketing Agency AI Automation Case Study
Background
Convert Cake is a digital marketing agency looking to expand its influencer marketing services, particularly focusing on TikTok influencers or KOLs in Thailand.
The agency wants to create a more efficient process for matching businesses with relevant TikTok influencers based on the client’s Facebook presence and website.
As an AI Engineer, your task is to develop an innovative solution that helps identify the most suitable TikTok influencers for Convert Cake’s clients.

Challenge: TikTok KOL Matcher for Thai Market
Your challenge is to develop an AI-powered tool that:
Analyzes a client’s Facebook page and website to understand their brand, audience, and content style
Identifies and evaluates relevant TikTok influencers or KOLs in Thailand who would be a good match for the client
Ranks potential influencers based on relevance, engagement metrics, and audience demographics
Presents a curated list of recommended TikTok KOLs with relevant profile information
This tool will help Convert Cake’s influencer marketing team quickly identify the most promising TikTok collaborators for clients looking to expand their presence in Thailand.

Primary Deliverable
Develop a working prototype of your TikTok KOL matching tool.
Your submission should include:

1. Working Application / Code
   Source code for your solution
   Setup instructions for running the application
   At minimum, the tool should output links to relevant TikTok profiles
   Include any data extraction, processing, and matching components
2. Loom Video Presentation
   Record a 5–10 minute Loom video walking through your solution.
   Your video should:
   Demonstrate the tool’s functionality with at least one example business
   Explain key technical decisions and implementation details
   Highlight potential business impact and future enhancements

Backup Option
If you are unable to complete a working prototype, provide a detailed written proposal including:

1. Solution Overview
   Outline your proposed solution
   Explain how AI would be integrated into the process
   Define the criteria your tool would use to evaluate and match influencers
2. Technical Implementation Plan
   Specify the tools, platforms, or APIs you would use for data collection and analysis
   Outline how you would develop the matching algorithm
   Include a basic flowchart or diagram of your system architecture
   Provide sample pseudocode for key components
   Address potential technical challenges and proposed solutions

Evaluation Criteria
Your solution will be assessed based on:
Practicality and feasibility of implementation
Technical understanding of AI, data extraction, and matching algorithms
Creativity and innovation in your approach
Clarity of communication and documentation
Consideration of business value and impact
Attention to ethical and privacy considerations

Resources

You are encouraged to suggest specific tools based on your knowledge and research.
Please include citations for any external resources used.
